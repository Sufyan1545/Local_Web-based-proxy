Local Web-based Proxy
A CroxyProxy-style web proxy that runs on your Android phone (Termux + Rammerhead) and opens from any device on the same Wi-Fi.
Type a link or a search, press Go, and the site opens inside the same page through the phone's connection.
Created by Sufyan with Claude.

Features
One page with a URL / search bar and a Go button. Sites open inside the page.
Tabs that survive closing the page or restarting the server.
Two tab views, switched with the ▦ / ▭ button under the search bar:
Rectangles: a tab strip, like a desktop browser.
Squares: cards with a live preview of each page, like Chrome's tab switcher. Tap a card to open it.
⟳ reload button, + new tab, ▲ / ▼ to hide or show the top bar.
Saved sessions: cookies and logins are kept on the phone for a year, so you stay signed in after the phone sleeps or the server restarts.
YouTube player: YouTube's own player is cut off after about a minute behind a rewriting proxy. So on a YouTube video page, the normal YouTube page stays (description, suggestions, comments, everything below) and a built-in player powered by yt-dlp is placed at the top, exactly over YouTube's own player area. Tap a suggested video and the player switches to it. The player has a quality menu (from 144p up to what the video offers, 1080p and more), speed (0.5x to 2x) and fullscreen, all behind the ⚙ button at its top-right corner, and it remembers your quality and where you stopped. Fullscreen fills the screen until you leave it. YouTube Shorts are left alone: they keep playing in YouTube's own player.
No password and no "create session" step.
